# FUPI pilot: participant consent (template, have a lawyer review before use)

I, ______________________ (name), agree to take part in a private, closed test of FUPI run by
______________________ (operator) from ____/____/____ to ____/____/____.

1. **What it is.** An experiment with offline digital cash. It is not a bank, a wallet licensed by
   the RBI, or a deposit. Nothing here is insured or guaranteed.
2. **My money.** I will put in at most Rs ______ (never more than I can afford to lose). The operator
   holds the matching amount in a bank account used only for this test.
3. **It can be lost.** The cash is the data on my device. If I lose the device or file, or share it, the
   money is gone and the operator is not obliged to replace it. I will keep my vault file private.
4. **Double-spending.** Offline payments are only final once the receiver settles them. If I spend
   the same token twice, the second receiver will not be paid and I may be asked to make it good.
5. **Getting out.** I can stop at any time. At the end (or on request) my unspent balance is paid back to my
   bank account within ______ days, less nothing: there are no fees.
6. **Data.** The operator keeps a log of amounts and payee labels (not my identity beyond the label I choose)
   for reconciliation. It will not be sold or shared, other than as the law requires.
7. **No public use.** I will not accept tokens from, or offer them to, anyone outside the group.
8. **Problems.** I will report anything odd (wrong balance, pending swap, a token that did not arrive) the same day.
